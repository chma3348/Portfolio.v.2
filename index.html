About me

Hey there! I’m Chris, born and raised in Colorado and recently back in the US after spending a few incredible years living in Sydney, Australia.

My love for photography started with a passion for travel, the outdoors, and chasing new experiences. I gave professional photography a try full-time, but I quickly realized it took the joy out of it. Being limited to certain types of work and clients made it feel more like a job than a creative outlet.

So I took a step back. These days, photography is still a big part of my life, but I approach it on my own terms. I take on projects that inspire me, work with people I genuinely enjoy, and charge what feels fair without the pressure of making a living from every shoot.

For me, photography is about connection. It's about people, places, and capturing meaningful moments. If you have a project in mind and want someone who brings creative energy and care to the process, let’s connect. I’d love to help bring your vision to life.

— Chris
